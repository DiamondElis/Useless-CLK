About the Useless Clock

What is a clock? What is its purpose? What data does it know? How does it share that data with you? How do you understand the data?

A useless clock visualizes the current time, just like a regular clock... but not in a way that anyone readily understands. It is a way to question the notion that we track time by a clock, and reacquaint ourselves with a different perspective of time.

This project reimagines timekeeping through the aesthetic of luxury dice watches, creating a beautiful but deliberately impractical way to visualize the passage of time.
